# Hey, I'm Josué 👋

**Electrical Engineering** student in Rio de Janeiro, bridging the gap between what happens in the circuit and what runs in the code.

- Power systems analysis (load flow, fault analysis, stability)
- Electrical machines and transformers
- Power electronics (converters, inverters, rectifiers)
- Transmission and distribution
- Power system protection
- Electrical installations and grounding
- Renewable energy and grid integration

**Control & Signals**
- Control systems: modeling, stability, time and frequency response
- Signals and systems

**Foundations**
- Circuit analysis: DC, sinusoidal steady state, transients
- Numerical methods: solving, in code, what has no closed-form solution
- Electromagnetics
